Propellers that mitigate tip vortex roll-up

Propeller tip vortices that hit downstream surfaces cause unsteady loads and noise. With CU Aerospace and the Aerodynamics and Unsteady Flows Research Group at Illinois, we tested propeller designs shaped to passively keep the tip vortex from rolling up into a coherent structure.

Facility
Subsonic wind tunnel, UIUC
Methods
Phase-locked stereo PIV, surface pressure
Partners
CU Aerospace, AUFRG

What I did

  • Collected phase-locked stereo PIV in the wakes of thrust-optimized and wake-optimized propellers to compare how their tip vortices form and roll up.
  • Designed an experiment to study how the rotor blade wake interacts with an airfoil placed downstream of the propeller test stand.
  • Measured the surface pressure distribution on the airfoil alongside the velocity fields.

T. Kopperstad, A. Borra, A. Beusse, C. Kim, E. Torres De Jesus, T. Saxton-Fox, P. Ansell. Design of propellers with passive mitigation of coherent tip vortex roll-up. AIAA SciTech Forum, 2023. doi
